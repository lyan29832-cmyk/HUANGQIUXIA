# PIC/S PE 009-17 to PE 009-18 change analysis

## Scope and evidence basis

Use this file when comparing PE 009-17 (25 August 2023) with PE 009-18 (24 September 2026). The comparison is based on the PE 009-18 Introduction revision history, the PIC/S official 23 September 2026 revision notice, the current PE 009-18 four-volume controlled set bundled in this Skill, and the archived official PE 009-17 Annexes text.

Official routes:

- PE 009-18 revision notice: `https://picscheme.org/en/news/revision-of-pics-gmp-guide-pe-009-18`
- Current publications: `https://picscheme.org/en/publications`
- Historical PE 009-17 Annexes: `https://picscheme.org/docview/8881`

## Executive conclusion

The official PE 009-18 revision history identifies the reason for revision as **Revision of Annex 19**. PIC/S states that the revision primarily concerns **Annex 19 Section 9** on reference and retention samples for parallel imported, parallel distributed and parallel traded products. No separate substantive revision to Part I, Part II or the other annexes was announced as part of PE 009-18.

Therefore, do not trigger a full-company gap assessment of every PE 009 chapter/annex solely because the version number changed. Perform a focused Annex 19 assessment, while updating the controlled regulatory index for the entire Guide.

## Annex 19 Section 9 clause mapping

### PE 009-17 position

PE 009-17 Section 9 contained two operational paragraphs:

- 9.1: where secondary packaging was not opened, only packaging material used needed to be retained because product mix-up risk was absent or low.
- 9.2: where secondary packaging was opened, e.g. carton/PIL replacement, one retention sample per packaging operation containing product was to be taken; the text highlighted the need to identify responsibility rapidly in case of mix-up because this could affect recall extent.

The section heading and applicability note referred to **parallel imported / parallel distributed products**.

### PE 009-18 position

PE 009-18 replaces the brief two-paragraph model with 9.1-9.5.4 and extends the substantive heading/applicability note to **parallel imported / parallel distributed / parallel traded products**.

| Topic | PE 009-17 | PE 009-18 | Compliance impact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Section 9 scope wording | Parallel imported / parallel distributed | Adds parallel traded | Applicability assessment should include the parallel-trade legal route where national law addresses it |
| Physical re-packaging materials | Only explicitly addressed through whether secondary packaging was opened | 9.1 expressly requires physical samples of labels, carton, PIL and other inserts for the shelf life of the re-packaged product | Sample inventory/SOP should identify each packaging-material specimen and retention duration |
| Reference sample of re-packaged product | Not expressly separated | 9.2 expressly says not required | Avoid creating unnecessary analytical reference-sample obligations for this specific repackaged-product context |
| Retention sample trigger | Required where secondary packaging opened; one per packaging operation | 9.3 requires a retention sample for each re-packaging operation | Requirement is clearer and more systematic; route through repackaging batch records |
| Retention period | No specific Section 9 duration stated | At least one year after expiry | SOP and archive controls need explicit duration |
| Sample representativeness | Product-containing sample where secondary packaging opened | Must represent marketed re-packaged product; normally primary + secondary package | Define minimum sample composition and market presentation |
| Secondary packaging unopened | Only packaging material used retained | Principle retained within 9.4 | Existing low-risk route remains, now integrated into the representativeness rule |
| Digital/photographic alternative | Not provided | 9.5 allows only if physical sample cannot reasonably be retained, duly justified, and agreed in advance with competent authority | Digital substitution is an exception, not a default option |
| Digital sample quality | Not provided | 9.5.1 requires complete record, full visual examination/investigation equivalence, high-quality images and batch packaging record traceability | Establish controlled capture, linkage and review procedure |
| Packaging data | Not provided | 9.5.2 requires data on primary and secondary packaging, e.g. batch no. and expiry | Image set/data package needs defined minimum content |
| Safety features / braille | Not provided | 9.5.3 requires evidence of safety features and braille identification | Photography must capture tamper/safety features and braille where applicable |
| Electronic record integrity | Not provided | 9.5.4 points to Annex 11 principles | Digital retention systems need lifecycle integrity, access, backup and retrieval controls |
| Recall linkage | Old 9.2 expressly described mix-up responsibility and recall-extent consequences | New text focuses on evidence/sample controls; recall linkage remains through broader GMP/PQS/Chapter 8 | Do not delete recall interfaces from company procedures merely because the explanatory sentence disappeared |

## What did not change in principle

The broader Annex 19 system still distinguishes reference samples from retention samples, requires traceability and appropriate storage, and retains general finished-product sample retention expectations. The PE 009-18 update should therefore be treated as a **targeted Section 9 enhancement**, not a wholesale redesign of Annex 19.

## Editorial differences to preserve, not silently correct

- Annex 19 Section 9 heading and the official revision notice include **parallel traded products**.
- Annex 19 Scope 1.3 and the Annexes table of contents still use wording focused on parallel imported/distributed medicinal products.

Record this as an official-text editorial consistency issue if it matters to a controlled gap assessment. Do not rewrite the regulator's text in a controlled citation.

## Recommended change-control assessment template

For an internal regulatory change assessment, capture at least:

1. source: PIC/S PE 009-18;
2. effective date: 24 September 2026;
3. superseded baseline: PE 009-17;
4. change owner: quality/regulatory function responsible for external standards;
5. affected text: Annex 19 Section 9;
6. legal applicability: whether the company performs/controls parallel imported/distributed/traded repackaging under applicable national law;
7. impacted procedures: reference/retention samples, packaging/repackaging, batch records, quality agreements, complaint/defect investigation, recall, data integrity, archive/record retention;
8. gap conclusion: applicable / not applicable / partially applicable;
9. actions, owners and due dates;
10. training and effectiveness check where procedures change.

If Section 9 is not applicable, document that conclusion with the scope/legal basis and still update the regulatory register to PE 009-18.
