# PIC/S PI 006-4: qualification and validation routing

## Evidence and status

Checked 2026-10-08. Official final PDF: https://picscheme.org/docview/11277 ; current index: https://picscheme.org/en/publications . PI 006-4 applies from 2026-10-01 and supersedes PI 006-3. It is inspection/industry guidance supporting Annex 15, not an Annex 15 replacement or automatic domestic law.

## Professional application workflow

For a live company assessment, first establish markets, competent authority, product category, registered process and validation state. Read full relevant final sections before a clause-level finding. For a PI 006-3-to-4 redline, inspect both versions; the routes above are not an exhaustive difference matrix.

Build an issue log with current SOP/protocol clause, external source, applicability, evidence gap, risk, proposed action, owner and completion criterion. Suggested workstreams: VMP governance; equipment/utilities qualification; process-validation lifecycle and CPV; cleaning strategy; data governance; change/deviation linkage. Use existing lifecycle references for PPQ/statistical design; do not invent a universal batch count.

Do not retrospectively invalidate completed studies solely due to the new document number. For periodic purified-water requalification, evaluate changes, maintenance, historical trends, excursions, seasons represented and residual uncertainty before choosing a protocol. Distinguish initial qualification examples from ongoing review/requalification. Training, an updated regulation list, or a passing PPQ batch alone does not prove a completed compliance assessment.
