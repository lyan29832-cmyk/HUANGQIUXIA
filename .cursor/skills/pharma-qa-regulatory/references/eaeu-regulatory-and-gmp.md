# EAEU pharmaceutical regulation and GMP

**October 2026 update routing:** Read [regulatory-update-2026-10.md](regulatory-update-2026-10.md) for the latest scoped version/status and applicability updates; earlier checkpoints below are historical. For qualification/validation use [pics-pi006-4-validation-map.md](pics-pi006-4-validation-map.md). Do not extend clinical electronic-data guidance to all CMC laboratories or sterile/advanced-product GMP to all oral-solid products.

## Purpose and activation

Read this reference for the Eurasian Economic Union (`EAEU`, Russian abbreviation `ЕАЭС`), the Eurasian Economic Commission (`EEC`, `ЕЭК`), EAEU GMP, EAEU pharmaceutical inspections, GMP certificates, third-country manufacturers, EAEU registration, or implementation by an EAEU Member State.

This is a routing and analysis guide, not a frozen copy of EAEU law. For current requirements, open the EAEU Legal Portal and the applicable Member State authority or inspectorate source at execution time.

## Establish scope before analysis

Identify all of the following when they can change the answer:

- medicinal product for human or veterinary use;
- finished product, active substance, investigational product, or another product category;
- lifecycle stage and procedure: development, clinical trial, registration, variation, manufacture, import, distribution, pharmacovigilance, or inspection;
- target Member State or States and whether a registration reference/recognition procedure is involved;
- manufacturer inside the Union or a production site in a third country;
- legal entity, exact production-site address, dosage form, product type, manufacturing operations, testing, packaging, storage, and batch-certification scope;
- inspection basis, applicant, competent authority or inspectorate, filing language, and intended certificate use.

At the verified baseline of 2026-09-04, the Member States reflected in the official EAEU certificate guidance are Armenia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, and the Russian Federation. Recheck membership if it is material to a later task.

## Keep the regulatory layers separate

Use this hierarchy:

1. Treaty-level and EEC acts on the EAEU Legal Portal, including the base decision, amendments, entry-into-force provisions, transitions, and an official consolidation where available.
2. EEC official implementation pages, common registers, forms, and adopted guidance, with their legal status stated.
3. The applicable Member State's law, authorized authority, inspectorate procedure, fees, portal, contract, and administrative requirements.
4. Inspectorate correspondence, application forms, contracts, and user-provided controlled copies for the defined project.
5. EU GMP, PIC/S, ICH, WHO, or secondary materials only for interpretation or comparison unless an EAEU or national act gives them a specific legal role.

Do not:

- use `Russia` and `EAEU` as synonyms;
- present one Member State's filing, legalization, payment, travel, or scheduling practice as an EAEU-wide rule;
- cite EU GMP or PIC/S as the legal basis for EAEU compliance merely because EAEU GMP is closely aligned;
- assume an old English translation incorporates later amendments;
- treat an EEC news item, draft, recommendation, or explanatory guideline as binding GMP text;
- assume that a China GMP certificate or another jurisdiction's inspection eliminates an EAEU inspection requirement;
- reintroduce the withdrawn historical PIC/S PE 009-12 baseline. If PIC/S comparison is requested, verify the current PIC/S version.

When an official English rendering is unclear or appears mistranslated, inspect the official Russian text and identify the translation limitation. Do not silently repair a legal title. Decision No. 83 paragraph 8 requires inspection submissions in Russian and/or, where required by Member State legislation, the state language of that Member State or a translation into those languages; determine the actual filing and working-language arrangement for the selected inspectorate.

## EAEU GMP architecture and interpretation

At the pre-2026-10-28 operative baseline, Decision No. 77 organizes EAEU GMP into:

- Part I: manufacture of finished medicinal products;
- Part II: active substances used as starting materials;
- Part III: GMP-related documents explaining regulatory expectations and practices;
- appendices for product types and specialized activities.

Council Decision 103 of 2026-09-09 adds Part IV with effect from 2026-10-28 and also amends general Part I wording. Apply the correct date-state; see the October update map.

Select all applicable parts and appendices. Do not review only Part I when active-substance manufacture, sterile manufacture, biological products, investigational products, computerized systems, qualification/validation, batch certification, reference/retention samples, or another specialized scope is relevant.

The core GMP system includes the pharmaceutical quality system, quality risk management, personnel and responsible roles, premises and equipment, documentation, production, quality control, outsourced activities, complaints and recalls, self-inspection, and product-quality review. Tie each observation to the clause and appendix actually in force for the defined scope.

The official English text uses `Qualified Person` and assigns EAEU/member-state responsibilities. Do not assume that an EAEU Qualified Person is legally interchangeable with an EU Qualified Person or a Chinese Quality Authorized Person merely because the same English abbreviation may be used. Map appointment, qualifications, continuous availability, batch-certification duties, delegation, and liability against the EAEU text and the selected Member State's law.

## Product quality review routing

For EAEU Product Quality Review work, use [product-quality-review-regulatory-map.md](product-quality-review-regulatory-map.md) together with the current consolidated text of EEC Council Decision No. 77. For finished medicinal products, Part I Chapter 1 paras. 1.10-1.11 contain the EU-aligned PQR structure: regular review of registered manufactured products including export-only products, normally annual documentation, the standard twelve review areas, evaluation by the manufacturer and registration-certificate holder where different, assessment of CAPA/revalidation, scientifically justified grouping, and agreement-defined responsibilities where the holder is not the manufacturer. For APIs, use Part II's Q7-aligned product-quality-review provisions.

Do not infer current EAEU legal status solely from textual similarity to EU/PIC/S. Recheck Decision No. 77, amendments, effective dates and any product-specific appendices on the EAEU Legal Portal before a definitive clause-level conclusion. At the 2026-09-30 PQR checkpoint, the official Decision No. 77 record could not be fetched in the execution environment; retain the Skill's previously verified Union-level routing baseline and mark currentness as not reverified if the official portal remains inaccessible.

## Intermediate and bulk hold-time routing

When the EAEU task concerns temporary storage of a finished-dosage intermediate or bulk product, maximum batch manufacturing time, pre-packaging bulk storage, transport during the hold, or an API intermediate, also read [intermediate-product-hold-time-regulatory-map.md](intermediate-product-hold-time-regulatory-map.md). Keep these EAEU layers separate:

- **EEC Council Decision No. 77**: binding EAEU GMP framework; use Part I for finished-dosage intermediates/bulk products, Part II for API intermediates, and applicable appendices.
- **EEC Board Decision No. 69 of 10 May 2018**, as currently amended: EAEU stability-study framework where hold/bulk storage creates stability-evidence questions.
- **EEC Board Recommendation No. 3 of 29 January 2019**: finished-dosage-form manufacturing/registration guidance. It contains the technically EU-aligned hold-time route, including the general `>30 days` solid-oral and `>24 h` sterile prolonged-storage markers and, where relevant, at least two pilot-scale batches. Preserve its status as a **Recommendation**; do not cite these numeric markers as clauses of Decision No. 77.
- **Decision No. 78 and current registration/variation rules**: determine whether a changed hold time/storage condition affects the approved EAEU dossier and what filing route applies.

For inter-site movement, include transfer time and conditions in the hold model when they form part of routine manufacture, and distinguish EAEU GDP/GMP transport controls from the scientific stability evidence used to support excursions. Revalidate the current Russian/consolidated text before quoting exact clauses or filing classifications.

## Pharmaceutical inspection of a third-country production site

Use the current version of Decision No. 83 and separate express rules from inspectorate implementation.

### Express Union-level controls identified in the official consolidation reviewed on 2026-09-04

- Paragraph 8: for a non-resident corporate manufacturer, the application package includes an inspection application, an SMF conforming to Part III of EAEU GMP, a duly certified copy or electronic copy of a valid manufacturing authorization/license or registry extract, evidence of compliance with the GMP applied by the manufacturing country if available, and a list of medicines manufactured or to be manufactured at the site.
- Paragraph 9: a third-country site may apply to the inspectorate of a Member State, subject to the Decision No. 78 route when the inspection is initiated as part of registration procedures. A documented refusal by one Member State's inspectorate can affect the route to another Member State.
- Paragraph 10: the process covers document acceptance/review, agreement on inspection dates, team formation, inspection program, site inspection and possible sampling/testing, reporting, CAPA-response assessment where required, the compliance decision, and certificate issuance or refusal.
- Paragraph 12: the inspection program is sent to the inspected entity at least 10 business days before the inspection starts.
- Paragraph 15: the inspection arrangement must support access within scope, review of premises, facilities, documents and records, interviews, workplace observation, and collection of evidence. Obstruction can lead to termination and a non-compliance outcome.
- Paragraph 19: reporting timelines depend on whether critical or material non-conformities are identified and whether response evaluation is required. Verify the current clause before fixing the project schedule.
- Paragraph 23: where critical or material non-conformities are identified, the inspected entity sends its response, CAPA plan, implementation report, and remedial evidence within 60 calendar days after receipt of Part I of the report.
- Paragraphs 21-22: the compliance decision depends on the presence and elimination of critical/material non-conformities, response timeliness, and cooperation with inspection rights.
- Paragraph 26: after a compliant decision, the authorized authority issues the certificate in the prescribed form within 10 business days under Member State legislation. The certificate covers only the dosage forms and manufacturing operations stated in it, reflects site status at inspection, and cannot exceed three years from completion of the inspection; risk principles can shorten it.
- Paragraphs 28-32: critical findings or later events can lead to certificate suspension, renewal, or termination, and status information is entered into Member State databases and the Union's integrated information system.

Revalidate paragraph numbers and wording against any later amendment before using them in a live submission or commitment.

### Non-conformity classification

Use the EAEU Decision No. 83 report-template terms for EAEU work:

| Chinese working term | EAEU official English term | Application caution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 严重不符合项 | `critical non-conformity` | Causes or leads to a significant risk of manufacturing a medicinal product hazardous to human life and health. Inspect the full current definition. |
| 重大不符合项 | `material non-conformity` | Do not silently replace with `major deficiency`; the reviewed EAEU English consolidation uses `material non-conformity`. Inspect all definition limbs and aggregation logic. |
| 其他不符合项 | `other non-conformity` | A deviation from EAEU GMP that is neither critical nor material. Do not equate automatically with a Chinese inspection grade. |

After a CAPA response, the status and number of non-conformities can be revised in Part II of the report. Keep the initial classification, response evidence, inspectorate evaluation, and final status traceable.

## Application and inspection-readiness method

Build one controlled matrix with these columns:

| EAEU source/clause | Member State source or inspectorate communication | Requirement status | Evidence/document | Exact entity/site/scope | Language/version | Owner | Gap/risk | Action and due date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Classify every line as one of:

- express EAEU requirement;
- Member State legal or administrative requirement;
- inspectorate contractual or procedural request;
- registration-party or customer request;
- professional readiness recommendation.

### Submission control for a foreign manufacturer

At minimum, verify:

- exact legal-entity name and production-site address across the application, manufacturing license, SMF, GMP evidence, product list, registration dossier, powers of attorney, and certificate request;
- requested dosage forms and manufacturing operations, including testing, primary/secondary packaging, storage, import-related activity, and batch certification where applicable;
- SMF conformity with the current EAEU Part III structure and accuracy of all annexes, layouts, flows, utilities, subcontractors, and product lists;
- current validity and complete scope of the manufacturing authorization/license and any domestic GMP evidence;
- whether certification, notarization, apostille/legalization, paper originals, electronic copies, and translations arise from the selected Member State's law or inspectorate instructions;
- filing language, onsite working language, interpreter qualifications, translation review, and version mapping;
- applicant, representative, PoA, consent, inspection contract, fees, travel/logistics, and submission channel against the selected authority's current procedure;
- availability of representative commercial or intended products and live operations during the proposed inspection window, without claiming this is universally mandatory unless supported by the authority's instructions.

Do not state that every EAEU inspection requires apostille, English versions of all quality documents, Russian versions of the full QMS, or a particular number of paper originals unless the current country-specific source or written request supports it.

### Onsite readiness

Cover the GMP system and the requested certificate scope, including:

- management responsibility, organization, independent quality oversight, and EAEU/member-state mapping of responsible roles;
- data integrity, document control, raw data, audit trails, computerized systems, and retrieval during inspection;
- deviation, OOS/OOT, root-cause investigation, CAPA, change control, validation/qualification, and effectiveness checks;
- supplier and outsourced-activity controls, technical agreements, and oversight evidence;
- batch documentation, product-quality review, stability, complaints, defects, recalls, returns, and distribution traceability;
- facility, utility, equipment, cleaning, contamination/cross-contamination control, and product-specific appendices;
- inspection data room, document index, site tour route, live-operation plan, interview preparation, interpreting protocol, photography/confidentiality handling, and rapid evidence reconciliation.

Document translation supports inspection but does not replace the controlled source record. Define which language version governs execution, how translations are approved or verified, how revisions stay aligned, and how interpreters handle technical terms and confidential information.

## EAEU-wide versus Member State or project-specific controls

| Layer | Typical content | How to report it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EAEU | GMP standard, unified inspection framework, report/certificate forms, registration framework, common databases | Cite the exact EEC act and current clause or annex |
| Member State | authorized authority/inspectorate, portal, national fees and timelines, administrative documents, accepted certification/legalization, national enforcement | Cite the current national source; name the state and authority |
| Inspectorate or contract | application checklist, contract, payment milestone, travel/logistics, proposed dates, document-exchange channel | Cite the written request or contract and label it procedural/contractual |
| Company project | responsible people, bilingual-document strategy, mock-inspection plan, production campaign, internal deadlines | Label as company control or professional recommendation |

If the applicant, registration holder, manufacturer, and local representative are different entities, map who owns the application, fees, communications, translations, commitments, CAPA response, and certificate maintenance. Do not infer those responsibilities from commercial custom.

## Output patterns

For an EAEU regulatory or inspection answer, prefer:

1. conclusion and defined scope;
2. source/status table separating EAEU and Member State bases;
3. `Regulatory text`, `Regulatory interpretation`, and `Professional assessment`;
4. submission or inspection-readiness matrix;
5. unresolved evidence gaps, owners, actions, and dates;
6. official sources with access date.

For certificate review, check the issuing authority, number, validity, last inspection date, legal entity, site address, human/veterinary scope, dosage forms, product types, manufacturing operations, testing, packaging, storage, batch certification, restrictions/notes, all pages, and database status.

## Second-pass EAEU QA checks

Before delivery, confirm that:

- the EAEU, EEC, Member State, authority, inspectorate, applicant, inspected entity, and representative are not conflated;
- the base decision, later amendments, effective/application dates, transition, and consolidation status were checked;
- human/veterinary, finished product/API, lifecycle, site, dosage form, and operation scope are correct;
- an English translation was not treated as current or controlling without verification;
- EAEU rules and Member State procedures are labeled separately;
- `critical`, `material`, and `other non-conformity` terminology is used consistently for Decision No. 83;
- certificate validity is not confused with certificate scope or current database status;
- project requests, professional recommendations, and regulatory requirements are clearly distinguished;
- all dates, periods, business/calendar-day distinctions, document versions, addresses, and names correspond across the output.
