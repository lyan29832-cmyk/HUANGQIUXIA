# QRM jurisdictional regulatory map - China / EU / US / PIC/S / EAEU

## Purpose and use

Use this file after [quality-risk-management.md](quality-risk-management.md) whenever the user asks whether a QRM principle is legally required, how Q9(R1) maps into a specific market, or how China, the EU, the United States, PIC/S and the EAEU differ.

This route was rechecked on **2026-09-29** for the principal QRM source identities below. Reopen the official current source before giving a clause-level or legal-currentness conclusion.

## Source hierarchy

Keep these layers separate:
1. **ICH Q9(R1):** harmonised technical guideline; Step 4 adoption does not itself create identical legal effect in every jurisdiction.
2. **Regional/national law or GMP:** determines enforceability and local obligations.
3. **Inspection guidance / Q&A / training:** supports interpretation and implementation but does not automatically have the same legal status as legislation or GMP rules.
4. **Company QRM procedure:** may be more detailed, but must not be presented as a regulator-mandated universal method unless the source says so.

## EAEU-specific reconciliation with Q9(R1)

At the 2026-09-29 official EAEU checkpoint, Decision No. 77 expressly requires a Pharmaceutical Quality System incorporating GMP and Quality Risk Management and states that QRM is a systematic process for assessment, control, communication and review of medicinal-product quality risk. Part III Chapter II provides the detailed QRM framework and is based on the older Q9-era model.

When EAEU text and Q9(R1) terminology differ:
- quote or paraphrase the EAEU requirement faithfully when stating the legal/GMP requirement;
- separately explain the Q9(R1) evolution where it improves current technical practice;
- do not claim that `Hazard Identification`, the R1 formality continuum, subjectivity controls or product-availability additions are EAEU legal wording unless a verified EAEU amendment/adoption establishes that;
- do not let legacy EAEU wording justify a weaker risk process where Q9(R1)-aligned controls are scientifically appropriate and compatible with the governing rules.

Official EAEU checkpoints:
- Decision No. 77 record: `https://docs.eaeunion.org/en/documents/306/2586/`
- Official English PDF linked from the Legal Portal: use the current portal copy and verify whether a later consolidated text or amendment applies.

## PIC/S-specific reconciliation with Q9(R1)

PE 009-18 entered into force on 24 September 2026. PIC/S stated that the 2026 revision concerns Annex 19, principally its section on parallel imported/distributed/traded products. Therefore, do not interpret the PE 009-17 to PE 009-18 version change as a wholesale update of QRM clauses.

For PIC/S QRM questions:
- use [pics-pe009-18-regulatory-map.md](pics-pe009-18-regulatory-map.md) for current guide routing;
- state the PE 009 text actually applicable to the topic;
- use Q9(R1) as an ICH technical reference only after checking the participating authority's adoption/implementation position;
- avoid presenting PIC/S as an independent MAH legal regime.

PIC/S checkpoint: `https://picscheme.org/en/publications`

## US-specific implementation overlay

FDA's Q9(R1) final guidance was issued in May 2023. FDA also issued Q8/Q9/Q10 Questions and Answers (R5) as Final Level 2 Guidance in May 2026. Use these as current Agency/ICH implementation guidance while distinguishing them from binding 21 CFR requirements.

FDA checkpoints:
- Q9(R1): `https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/q9r1-quality-risk-management`
- Q8/Q9/Q10 Q&A R5: `https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/q8-q9-and-q10-questions-and-answers-r5`

## China / EU / holder-risk boundary

When the question concerns MAH/holder lifecycle risk governance rather than a single QRM exercise, also load [mah-holder-risk-management-regulatory-map.md](mah-holder-risk-management-regulatory-map.md). Do not merge:
- pharmaceutical quality risk management;
- pharmacovigilance/product benefit-risk management;
- statutory MAH/applicant/manufacturer responsibilities.

## Decision rule for cross-jurisdiction answers

For each material statement, identify:
- jurisdiction;
- source type and legal status;
- product/lifecycle scope;
- exact current source/version;
- whether the statement is explicit regulatory text, interpretation, or professional QRM practice.

If Q9(R1) is technically newer than a jurisdiction's incorporated GMP/QRM text, present both layers rather than silently treating them as identical.
