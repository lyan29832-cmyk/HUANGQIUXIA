# Technology transfer: completeness review and translation controls

## Checkpoint and boundary
Reviewed 2026-10-08. The module covers the principal lifecycle workflow for translating and reviewing pharmaceutical transfer SOPs/SMPs, plans, protocols and reports. This is functional readiness, not certification that every country's requirements are collected or that a particular company's document, transfer or regulatory submission is approved. Apply the regulatory map and project-specific currentness/adoption review.

## Terminology and source priority
Use the priority in translation-and-style.md and the controlled CS snapshot. Do not modify historical terminology TSVs to imply a new user approval. The following are contextual working recommendations unless already confirmed in the controlled terminology; reconcile with definitions in the supplied document.

| Chinese concept | English working rendering | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 技术转移 / 知识转移 | technology transfer / knowledge transfer | Use technical transfer where an official source or controlled title uses it; avoid unneeded alternation. |
| 转出方 / 接收方 | sending unit (SU) / receiving unit (RU) | Define abbreviations once; preserve the document's approved names. |
| 工艺转移 / 分析方法转移 | process transfer / analytical method transfer | Transfer of a manufacturing process is distinct from transfer of its test methods. |
| 转移计划 / 转移方案 / 转移报告 | transfer plan / transfer protocol / transfer report | A plan governs work; a protocol specifies execution and acceptance; use the source's actual function. |
| 接收方能力评估 / 差距评估 | receiving-unit capability assessment / gap assessment | Do not convert a preliminary assessment into qualification or approval. |
| 确认 / 验证 / 核实 | qualification / validation / verification, according to context | Equipment qualification, method validation and compendial-method verification are distinct; do not apply a single mechanical mapping. |
| 工艺性能确认 | process performance qualification (PPQ) | Distinguish PPQ from the whole process-validation lifecycle. |
| 持续工艺确认 | continued process verification (CPV) | Existing CS terminology; continuous process verification and EU ongoing process verification require their own context and definitions. |
| 转移验收标准 / 转移验收 | transfer acceptance criteria / transfer acceptance | Not automatically a batch-release specification, regulatory approval or batch disposition. |
| 方法可比性 / 生物制品可比性 | method comparability / biological product comparability | Interlaboratory method assessment is not itself an ICH Q5E product comparability exercise. |
| 控制策略 / 可接受标准 | control strategy / acceptance criteria | Preserve parameter limits, conditions and applicability. |
| 偏差 / 遗留行动 / 转移关闭 | deviation / outstanding action / transfer closure | Closure requires documented evidence and authorized disposition, not merely completion of a report. |

The CS snapshot contains a potential organizational-name conflict: 技术转移中心质量保证部 is paired with “Quality Assurance Department of Early Research and Development System” / RDQA. Do not mechanically apply that pair to a technology-transfer department. Check the supplied document's organizational structure or user-confirmed title and flag an unresolved mismatch. This is a contextual conflict flag, not authorization to overwrite the controlled snapshot.

## Translation workflow and acceptance
1. Establish document type, approved definitions, roles, markets, lifecycle stage and references. Search CS terminology before selecting translations. Preserve numbering, tables, attachments, document codes, revision history and cross-references.
2. Translate every applicable section and cell. Preserve obligation, negation, exceptions, prerequisites, responsibility, sequence, quantities, units, sampling design, acceptance limits and statistical expressions. Do not add assumed sample sizes, batches, equivalence margins or abbreviations.
3. Separately review technical meaning: WHO 12.24 places implementation of testing procedures at the receiving unit before testing process-validation samples; EU Chapter 6.37 requires reviewing original validation and gaps before method transfer. Do not silently repair a source document that reverses a prerequisite. Translate faithfully and issue an explicit review finding/proposed correction.
4. Independently proofread against the source for completeness, terminology, grammar, roles, numerical accuracy and internal logic. Check SU/RU responsibilities; plan/protocol/report; validation/verification/qualification; CPV meanings; transfer acceptance/batch release; method/product comparability; risk justification versus a claimed waiver.
5. Deliver the corrected translation and a separate unresolved-issue list where needed. Do not declare document approval, regulatory compliance or successful transfer from translation alone. Apply established user style rules, including This SMP where prescribed and ordinary professional nouns without unsolicited bold emphasis.

## Example forward check
Input: “接收方完成分析方法转移并批准报告后，方可检测工艺验证样品。转移验收不代表批放行。” Expected meaning: the receiving unit must complete analytical method transfer and obtain report approval before testing process-validation samples; transfer acceptance does not constitute batch release. Preserve both prerequisites and the second sentence's negation. This company instruction is stronger than a bare guidance summary; do not weaken it during translation or label the report-approval condition a verbatim WHO clause.
