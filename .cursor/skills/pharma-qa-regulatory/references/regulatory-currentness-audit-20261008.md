# Expanded regulatory currentness audit — 2026-10-08

## Scope and interpretation

Use this checkpoint to route currentness questions and prevent stale-copy reliance. Read the accompanying [structured audit](regulatory-currentness-audit-20261008.json) for all 113 pre-existing URL checks, 15 controlled PDFs, 51 document-family conclusions and the 195-country working coverage list. The counts are inventory/retrieval counts, not a number of laws certified as current.

Do not say “all countries/all regulations verified.” No country is certified as having its complete applicable pharmaceutical legal universe closed. China, EU, US, EAEU, PIC/S and ICH received core-source review; UK, Japan, Canada, Australia, Singapore and WHO received selected core-source expansion. EU/EAEU Union rules do not close member-state implementation law. Other country/territory regulatory frameworks remain unverified. The country list is a working index, not a statement of sovereignty or a complete list of independent regulatory jurisdictions; add Hong Kong, Macao, Taiwan and other separately regulated territories as needed.

Separate four checkpoints: document identity/version; readable official operative text; complete amendment/repeal chain and effective/transition dates; domestic adoption/applicability. A working URL or an official-index listing closes none of the other checkpoints by itself. Mark unclosed checkpoints explicitly; do not inherit historical “verified” labels as current conclusions.

The first URL sweep was followed by individual official-source reopening at line 0. Results: 76 key-text retrievals, 33 blocked/empty responses, 2 index-framework responses and 2 non-regulatory publication records. A successful key-page retrieval is not full-document/annex reading. Preserve valid same-day evidence already inspected even if a later retry is rate-limited; identify that evidence and the retry limitation.

## Open evidence gates

Prioritise the 2021 change-acceptance copy, CDE 34/44/45 final annexes, current EUR-Lex consolidations, EMA defect/rapid-alert PDFs, EAEU consolidated amendment chains, country adoption conflicts and all unreviewed controlled China change-management sources. Keep the full reference set inspectable; do not delete historical user copies or silently relabel them as current.

For a genuinely global project, establish a country/jurisdiction × product/process × lifecycle × legal-layer applicability inventory using the enterprise's real markets and activities. Retrieve each official current full text and amendments, retain exact evidence, reconcile local law/permit/inspection/reporting rules and close entries individually. Until then, report completed scope and remaining gaps rather than a global compliance or completeness certificate. The accompanying audit records all country-complete flags as unclosed.
