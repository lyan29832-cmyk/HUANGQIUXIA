## Market and lifecycle decision record
Build one row per market and affected operation. Record product category, applicant/MAH/manufacturer legal roles, sending and receiving site, operation/scale/equipment/method/material/packaging differences, registered baseline, applicable rule and current version, submission or notification route, research requirements, implementation condition, release condition, responsible RA person, evidence and approval. Leave unresolved classifications open rather than selecting a route from a generic site-change label.

Distinguish clinical supply, registration/PPQ material and routine commercial supply. An approved transfer report does not establish permission to manufacture, supply clinical material, sell or release a batch. Confirm permits, filings, approvals, commitments and batch-disposition authority separately.

## Clauses versus professional controls
- WHO technical guidance supports due diligence, staged management and documented receiving-unit capability. It does not confer national licensing or approval.
- ICH Q10 provides a lifecycle quality-system model; additional elements beyond regional GMP are not automatically new binding requirements.
- EU Chapter 6 links transfer to dossier/method validation review, an approved detailed protocol, acceptance criteria and investigated deviations. Training alone cannot demonstrate method suitability.
- EU Annex 15 5.19 requires justified design; 5.20 recognizes three consecutive routine batches as generally acceptable in the traditional approach, allows justified alternatives and may require additional ongoing data. Do not rewrite this as either an unconditional global minimum or complete freedom to ignore regional expectations.
- China GMP links validation, receiving capability, necessary information and supervision. Consult the current validation annex and product-specific guidance before a batch-number conclusion; no global-number shortcut.
- Stage gates, suggested owners, numerical project KPIs and the templates in this Skill are professional implementation tools unless individually supported by an applicable cited requirement.
