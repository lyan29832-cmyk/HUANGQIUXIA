## Contents

## Use boundary
These are professional assessment checklists, not verbatim regulatory requirements or fixed acceptance criteria. Select applicable items; record why omitted items are not applicable. Apply product-specific and market-specific requirements using technology-transfer-regulatory-map.md. Never infer comparability or waive studies solely because finished-product release results pass.

## Product/operation modules
| Module | Differences and knowledge to assess | Evidence to request / decisions to document |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oral solids | API form/particle properties, excipient functionality, blending/segregation, granulation endpoint, drying, milling, compression/dwell time, coating, humidity/static effects, yield and holds | Unit-operation and scale/equipment bridge; content uniformity/dissolution/impurity evidence; packaging/stability/hold support; engineering runs as justified. Match equipment functionality, not just nominal volume. |
| API | Synthetic/biological route, impurity fate/purge, solvent/catalyst residues, crystallization/polymorph, particle size, drying, intermediates, reprocessing/rework and recovered solvents | Material/source and process history; impurity/control strategy; hold/retest basis; receiving cleaning/containment; applicable Q7/Part II evidence. Do not import finished-dose release logic uncritically. |
| Sterile product | Contamination-control strategy, sterilization or aseptic process, bioburden/endotoxin, filter compatibility/integrity, aseptic holds/interventions, environmental monitoring, freeze-drying and container closure | Receiving-site CCS and qualification; sterilization/filtration and aseptic-process simulation readiness as applicable; microbiological method suitability; transport/hold and closure evidence. Small-molecule oral-solid experience does not demonstrate sterile expertise. |
| Biologic | Cell bank/raw-material history, upstream/downstream conditions, resin/membrane lifecycle, clearance, potency, heterogeneity, aggregation, adsorption/shear, freeze/thaw | Fit-for-purpose analytical characterization; process/material comparability; stability and viral-safety strategy as applicable; appropriate Q5E/other guideline routing. Clinical bridging is a separate case-specific regulatory question. |
| ADC | Antibody, linker/payload and conjugation interfaces, drug-to-antibody ratio/distribution, free payload, potency, impurities, containment and sterile formulation | Traceability across partners; conjugation/quench/purification controls; analytical/potency readiness; site-specific cleaning/HBEL and operator protection; sterile fill/lyophilization/stability/transport bridge. No platform-level assumption of equivalence. |
| Packaging/device combination | Primary-contact materials, barrier and closure, line configuration, coding/artwork, reconciliation, device function if applicable | Packaging qualification, compatibility/stability and label/version control; device-specific quality/regulatory pathway where applicable; shipping verification. |
| Cleaning/cross-contamination | Equipment surface/geometry, product residues and degradants, detergents, recovery, dirty/clean holds, campaigns and shared facilities | Receiving-site cleaning strategy and validation basis; hazard/HBEL assessment where appropriate; residue method suitability. SU cleaning evidence is knowledge input, not automatic RU validation. |
| Digital/data transfer | Recipes, formulas, access roles, method processing/integration, audit trails, metadata, record format and migrations | Controlled configuration/version, intended-use validation/verification, reconciliation and data traceability; do not accept PDF summaries as a replacement for necessary raw electronic data. |

## MAH and CDMO interfaces
Create a responsibility/interface matrix and align quality agreement, project plan and actual procedures. Resolve contradictions before execution.

| Interface | Define and retain evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Parties and scope | Legal entity/site, delegated operations, methods/materials, target markets, permitted subcontractors, MAH Quality Head oversight and actual statutory release roles. |
| Technical baseline | Approved process/method/specification versions, change-control IDs, dossier commitments, allowable ranges and who may authorize modifications. |
| Data and knowledge | Ownership/licence/access, source raw data and metadata, audit trails as relevant, retention, retrieval and continuing access after termination. Confidentiality/IP terms cannot obstruct required quality evidence. |
| Deviations/OOS/OOT | Notification trigger and contractually defined timing; investigation leadership, source access, MAH review, batch/other-product impact, escalation and CAPA. Do not invent a universal statutory 24-hour clock. |
| Changes | Prior notification/approval obligations by impact, RA assessment, emergencies, retrospective unauthorized changes and regulatory commitments. |
| Batches and release | Material status, manufacturing review, quality certification/release, clinical/commercial disposition, validation-batch restrictions, and authority under each jurisdiction. Transfer acceptance is a separate decision. |
| Audit and inspection | Initial capability assessment, risk-based ongoing audits, inspection notice/outcome exchange and CAPA access; scope covers relevant subcontractors. |
| Lifecycle monitoring | Stability, CPV, PQR/APR source data and ownership, adverse trends, complaints/recalls and supply interruption. |
| Exit and continuity | Records/samples/standards and knowledge handover, materials disposition, residual actions, alternative site and supply continuity; retain legal obligations after contract end. |

## Personnel and knowledge continuity
Use demonstration, joint execution, troubleshooting drills and documented competency outcomes. Define process/method experts and backups; capture decision rationale and known failure modes. If key personnel may leave during a long project, plan overlap and handover rather than equating attendance records with mastery. A paper-based controlled system can be suitable; do not presume EDMS/eQMS is mandatory or fabricate an existing digital platform.
