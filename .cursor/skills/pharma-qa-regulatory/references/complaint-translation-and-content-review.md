## Content-review matrix
For each row record source clause, existing document clause, status (covered / partial / missing / conflicting / not applicable / not verified), consequence, proposed text, owner and unresolved evidence. Cite legal requirements separately from professional recommendations.

| Area | Review questions |
| --- | --- |
| Scope and actors | Marketed or investigational, finished product or API, MAH/manufacturer/distributor? Include group subsidiaries and outsourced operations where applicable; do not force retail rules onto MAH non-retail self-sales. |
| Intake and registration | All relevant channels; original allegation and receipt timestamp; incomplete/anonymous complaints assessed using available information; missing sample or batch cannot automatically terminate risk assessment. Duplicate records linked while preserving source information and earliest qualifying awareness. |
| Responsibilities | QA authority; designated investigator; Quality Authorized Person notification; MAH Quality Head, RA, PV, Medical, manufacturing, sales, logistics and contractor interfaces; backup/escalation outside office hours. Central group handling must not delay site/market action. |
| Classification and triage | Quality/non-quality coding, potential defect, severity, investigation depth, safety/PV, falsification and transport routes distinguished. Q/O codes are administrative classification, not a health-risk judgement or recall class. Reclassify as evidence changes. |
| Initial controls and external clocks | Quarantine/stop shipment/sale/use where justified; correct trigger, first awareness, reporting actor, recipient, days versus working days and local calendar. Do not await full RCA or shift day 0 through internal forwarding. |
| Investigation | Original claim, hypothesis, relevant manufacturing/packaging/QC/stability/storage/transport records; retain and complaint samples; related lots/products/sites/components/markets; recurrence; evidence-based root cause or explicit uncertainty. China quality-defect complaints cannot use the US no-investigation exception as a blanket waiver. |
| Sample and data integrity | Traceability, chain of custody, condition, testing purpose/criteria, quantity limits, destructive testing, custody/disposition, data access and paper/electronic controls. A passing retain sample does not refute every distributed-unit defect. |
| PV/medical interface | Prompt parallel transfer of safety information; death/group-event emergency paths; actual PV case-validity/reportability rules; quality support and final reconciliation. PV timelines do not authorize delaying urgent quality controls. |
| Recall and authority reporting | Suspected defect versus confirmed defect; FAR/BPDR/PV/quality-defect reports and recall assessed separately. Internal complaint severity is not a statutory recall class; a recall notification does not substitute for FAR. |
| CAPA and closure | Cause evidence, immediate correction, systemic CAPA where needed, owners/dates/effectiveness; customer response; regulatory updates; residual risk. Complaint closure and long-term CAPA effectiveness may have separate linked statuses, not a false completed/effective flag. |
| Trend and system effectiveness | Product/batch/category/market recurrence and normalized trends where useful; all assessed outcomes remain trendable; PQR/APR, management review, repeat CAPA and system-effectiveness feedback. |
| Records and forms | Local retention rules; access/confidentiality; master complaint ID, investigation/PV/CAPA/recall links, version control and numbering reconciliation across registration form, classification form, log and reports. ## Translation controls and approved conventions
Use the source priority in translation-and-style.md. Confirmed CS titles include Product Complaint Management Procedure, Complaint Information Registration Form, Complaint Classification and Preliminary Investigation Form, Complaint Log, Interim Report on Complaint Expanded Investigation, Complaint Expanded Investigation Report, and Customer Complaint Response Template. Preserve these controlled titles unless the user approves a change.

Later confirmed company conventions: complaint receipt identifier DC—Year—SN; classification identifier category–last two year digits–three-digit serial number, Q for quality/O for other (examples Q-20-001 and O-20-001). Do not silently reformat an approved numbering system or conflate receipt and classification identifiers. The registration-form target “complete within one working day of receipt” is an internal documentation target; immediate escalation/PV/regulatory clocks can precede form completion.

Other contextual renderings: complainant / 投诉人; recipient / 接收人; complaint source / 投诉来源; product quality complaint / 产品质量投诉; quality defect / 质量缺陷; complaint investigation / 投诉调查. Prefer complete terms over INFO in a proposed polished form, while preserving quoted source wording. Keep complaint validity/substantiation, defect confirmation and root-cause certainty in separate fields. Not substantiated is not automatically false or invalid. Translate recall according to the governing jurisdiction, not as a synonym for all returns. Preserve shall/should/may, negation, exceptions, all/any, timing and role assignments; use Quality Management Specialist and MAH Quality Head where prescribed. Chinese Quality Authorized Person, EU Qualified Person and PIC/S Authorised Person require legal-role context rather than assumed equivalence. Do not bold ordinary nouns in running text.

## Combined translation and content-review workflow
1. Establish source version, document type, applicable entities/products/markets and related procedures/forms; load controlled terminology. If only a fragment is supplied, review that fragment and label unassessed sections.
2. Review source content against the matrix, clauses and evidence. Identify factual omissions, unsafe sequencing, contradictory duties, inadequate investigation/closure criteria and numerical errors.
3. Produce a faithful translation with matching numbering/tables/attachments and independently proofread it against the source. Do not silently replace a technically deficient source sentence with a corrected one in the translation.
4. Provide review findings with original wording, issue, applicable source/status, risk and proposed corrected Chinese/English wording. Label a proposed revision separately from a faithful translation. If asked to revise the document, apply authorized corrections transparently and reconcile both language versions.
5. Deliver three clearly identified outputs as appropriate: corrected translation; content-review/issue matrix; proposed revised wording. Only present a final approved document after unresolved critical factual/role/market issues have been resolved by the authorized owner; translation QA alone is not corporate approval.

## Forward-test safeguards
Test an SMP that requires investigation completion before PV referral, calls complaints invalid whenever retains pass, or resets the US FAR clock when headquarters receives a contractor report. The review must identify each defect and preserve the original meaning in the faithful translation. For a group event or death case, do not reduce immediate reporting to a routine 15-day target. For a non-quality code, still screen embedded safety/quality information. Do not claim EAEU/all-country complete currentness from the China/US/EU/PIC/S core review.
