## Template controls
Use these schemas to create project-specific controlled documents, not as completed records. Leave unknown facts as [to confirm]; mark not-applicable items with rationale. Do not fabricate execution data, approvals, dates, acceptance limits or company procedures. Tailor bilingual wording to the controlled CS terminology. When requested, create editable Word/Excel outputs using the relevant artifact workflow. Professional templates do not override law, approved dossier, quality agreement or company SOP.

## T5 gate review and decision
| Gate | Minimum evidence question | Proposed decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G0 initiation | Is scope governed, legally feasible and knowledge access secured? | Pass / conditional / hold |
| G1 execution readiness | Are relevant gaps controlled, people/equipment/materials ready, and approved protocols/data controls available? | Pass / conditional / hold |
| G2 PPQ readiness | Are process/control strategy, critical qualification, analytical readiness, materials, protocol/design and regulatory interfaces sufficient for intended PPQ? | Pass / conditional / hold |
| G3 transfer acceptance | Do complete data establish RU capability and criteria achievement; are deviations, documents, training and residual risk disposition adequate? | Pass / conditional / hold |
| G4 sustainment/closure | Are responsibilities, regulatory actions, monitoring and residual-action tracking transferred and accepted? | Close / retain open |

Record scope of permission; evidence IDs; open items; consequence; owner/deadline; conditions; monitoring; decision-maker and date. Conditional passage is allowed only where scientifically and legally defensible; do not waive required approvals, critical safety controls, necessary method capability or missing evidence that invalidates the planned decision. A nonblocking administrative action can remain open with accountable tracking; record that distinction explicitly.
