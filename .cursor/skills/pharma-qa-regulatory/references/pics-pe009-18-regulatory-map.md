## Annex 19 - current PE 009-18 control points

### General Annex 19 controls

The core Annex 19 framework remains: reference samples support analytical testing; retention samples preserve a specimen of the fully packaged finished product; finished-product reference/retention samples are generally retained at least one year after expiry; traceability, storage conditions, written agreements and competent-authority access remain important.

### Revised Section 9 - parallel imported/distributed/traded products

Apply Section 9 only where national legislation addresses parallel imported, parallel distributed or parallel traded products. Do not generalise this section to ordinary commercial manufacture or repackaging outside that legal context.

PE 009-18 Section 9 now establishes that:

1. Physical samples of packaging materials used in re-packaging, such as labels, cartons, PILs and other inserts, should be retained for the shelf life of the re-packaged finished product.
2. Reference samples of the re-packaged product are not required.
3. A retention sample should be taken for each re-packaging operation and retained at least one year after expiry.
4. The retention sample should represent the product released to market and should include primary and secondary packaging; if the secondary packaging is not opened, retaining only the packaging material used is sufficient.
5. A photographic/digital sample may replace the physical retention sample only where physical retention cannot reasonably be achieved, the approach is duly justified, and the competent authority agrees in advance.
6. A photographic/digital sample must support visual examination/investigation equivalent to a physical sample, use high-quality photographs, and be traceable to the batch packaging record.
7. The photographic/digital sample should capture data on primary and secondary packaging, including batch number and expiry date, show application of safety features, and allow identification of braille information.
8. Electronic photographic/digital samples should follow Annex 11 principles to preserve record integrity for the retention period.

### Editorial consistency caution

The substantive Section 9 heading and the PIC/S revision notice include **parallel traded products**. However, the Annex 19 Scope 1.3 and the Annexes table of contents continue to use older wording referring to parallel imported/distributed products. Treat this as an editorial consistency point in the official PE 009-18 text; do not alter the controlled source. For applicability, use the actual Section 9 text together with the PIC/S revision notice and relevant national legislation.

## Practical company impact assessment

For a routine finished-dosage manufacturer that does not perform parallel import/distribution/trade repackaging, the PE 009-18 version update normally requires:

1. update the external-regulation register from PE 009-17 to PE 009-18;
2. document applicability of revised Annex 19 Section 9;
3. if Section 9 is not applicable, retain a documented non-applicability conclusion rather than rewriting all reference/retention sample procedures;
4. verify that existing Annex 19 controls outside Section 9 remain correctly implemented;
5. if parallel trade/repackaging is applicable, revise sample-retention SOPs, quality agreements, data-integrity controls for digital samples, and competent-authority interaction procedures;
6. check downstream SOPs for QC samples, complaints, quality defects, recalls and batch documentation because Annex 19 samples can become investigation evidence.

This is professional implementation guidance. The legal applicability of parallel trade rules depends on the relevant national/regional legislation.
