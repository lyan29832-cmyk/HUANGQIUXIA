修订文件生效

外部审计、自检后修订的文件，中文版和英文版一起生效。生效日为批准日。从批准日起用新版，旧版停止使用。

中文版和英文版写的是同一要求。两版不一致时，先按中文版执行，并立即改正英文版。

正在使用的记录：

1. 只改了版式或语句，记录项目和含义没变：已经在用的旧记录可以写完再归档。新开的记录用新版。
2. 增加、删除或调整了记录项目，或者含义变了：旧记录停止填写。未完成的内容抄入新版，写明抄录日期和旧记录编号，并与旧记录核对一致。旧记录归档，之后只填新版。

同一份记录不得混用新旧版。

Effective date of revised documents

Documents revised after an external audit or a self-inspection take effect together with their Chinese and English versions. The effective date is the approval date. Use the new version from that date. Stop using the old version.

The Chinese and English versions state the same requirements. If they differ, follow the Chinese version and correct the English version at once.

Records already in use:

1. Layout or wording only; recorded items and their meaning unchanged: finish the old record, then archive it. Start new records on the new version.
2. Recorded items added, deleted, or changed, or their meaning changed: stop the old record. Copy unfinished information to the new version, note the copy date and the old record number, and check it against the old record. Archive the old record. Use only the new version after that.

Do not mix the old and new versions on the same record.
